# Limitations

Known gaps and simplifications. The full version is written in task D7; until then
every gap found during the build is added here.

* **Sparkod in the address.** Anyone who gets the link can open that progress. Accepted because no personal data is stored; the "Om appen och integritet" page says so.
* **PostgreSQL not yet verified end to end.** Storage and the learner flow were verified locally on SQLite. The check against Supabase PostgreSQL is the owner's deployment step after D1.5.
* **Settings actions not yet in the interface.** "Om appen och integritet" mentions resetting and deleting progress under Inställningar. The service functions exist (D1.4); the page is still a placeholder.
* **Moms rate date.** The legal rule uses the date of the beskattningsgrundande händelse; the engine uses the date of the transaction given to it. For the exercises these are the same date.
* **Momsomföring in öre.** `vat.settlement` moves the exact amount in öre to 2650. Rounding the momsdeklaration to whole kronor with öresutjämning on 3740 is not modelled.
* **Moms report period.** `vat.report` and `vat.settlement` count verifikationer by date within the given period and leave out omföringar; the caller must pass the period bounds, otherwise the whole journal is one period.
* **EU trade.** Only purchases and sales between businesses under the huvudregel, at 25 procent, are modelled. Trepartshandel, kedjetransaktioner, sales to private persons (OSS), the periodisk sammanställning and the 12 and 6 procent rates on EU purchases are not.
* **Payroll.** Skatteavdrag is an input; tax tables are not modelled. Only the full arbetsgivaravgift is used; age based reductions and växa-stöd are not. Avgifter are computed in öre on the summed bruttolön, while the arbetsgivardeklaration works in whole kronor. The payment voucher moves money from the bank to the skattekonto (1630); the debitering of 2710 and 2731 against 1630 is a separate verifikation.
* **Depreciation.** Straight line per month only, with each month rounded down to whole öre and the remainder in the last month. Residual value, half year conventions and degressive plans are not modelled.
* **Bankavstämning matching.** Lines match only one to one on an equal amount within three days. A bank line that covers several ledger entries (or the reverse) is reported as unmatched.
* **Reports before the bokslut.** `balance_sheet` adds the period's result to Årets resultat because no closing entries (bokslutsverifikationer) are made. Lagerförändring is not booked: purchases of handelsvaror are expensed in full and the varulager keeps its ingående balans. Subheadings such as Anläggningstillgångar, Omsättningstillgångar and Kortfristiga skulder are not yet part of the report objects. Account 4535 (tjänster från EU) is placed under Råvaror och förnödenheter by its kontoklass; a service that is an overhead would belong under Övriga externa kostnader.
* **Recipe simplifications.** The skatteavdrag in the payroll recipes is a chosen share of the bruttolön (28, 30 or 32 procent) rounded down to whole kronor, not a skattetabell. The hyra recipe has no moms (a landlord that is not frivilligt skattskyldig). Momsbetalning is the debitering on the skattekonto (debet 2650, kredit 1630); the transfer from the bank to the skattekonto is the betalning_skattekonto recipe. Utlägg is credited to 2890 Övriga kortfristiga skulder, with 2820 Kortfristiga skulder till anställda accepted. Exercise dates fall in 2026.
* **Mastery and XP choices.** Only the first attempt on an exercise instance counts for mastery and XP, because the feedback after it shows the solution. A miss resets the repetition count, so a Befäst skill can fall back to Kompetent. The XP thresholds for the levels (250, 750, 1 500, 3 000) are the app's own choice; PRD section 9 names the levels only. Today's date for due dates and the svit is taken in Swedish time (Europe/Stockholm).
* **Hitta felet and Avstämning.** A planted wrong amount changes two rows, the largest row and its counterpart on the other side, because a booked verifikation must balance. Avstämning uses only customer and supplier payments plus one bank fee, one month at a time, and its differences are always one missing fee and one transposed amount.
* **Exercise grid.** An empty account cell in the verifikation grid shows Streamlit's grey placeholder "None", which cannot be changed in Streamlit 1.67; the grid therefore starts with one row and a caption explains how to type. `st.data_editor` is hard to use on a phone; a form fallback is planned for D7.4.
* **Lessons are drafts.** All 20 lessons have status utkast until the owner's review in D7.1, so the Lektioner, Övningar and Repetition pages show them only when `RA_SHOW_DRAFTS=1` is set. The rule entries are published because each was checked at its source.
* **Fixed items without new numbers.** Klassificering and resonemang items are written in the content files and give the same question every time; only recipe based exercises get new numbers. Their illustrative amounts (for example in the förbrukningsinventarier classification) are not keys computed by the engine.
* **Lesson completion.** A lesson counts as done when every Kontroll item has been answered correctly at least once, in any attempt. Reading the lesson or doing the Gör exercises does not mark it done.
* **Dated figures in lesson text.** The arbetsgivaravgift 31,42 procent, the prisbasbelopp 59 200 kronor and the K2 periodisering limit of 7 000 kronor are written in lesson and rule text for 2026. The engine reads rates from the dated content files, but this text has to be updated by hand when the figures change.
* **Simulator month.** The scenario is one month, January 2026, so the ingående balanser hold no result accounts. They also hold no skulder for December's moms or December's skatteavdrag and arbetsgivaravgifter, so no payment to the skattekonto falls in the month; January's skatteavdrag and arbetsgivaravgifter stay as skulder at the month's end. Lagerförändring is not booked (see Reports). Salaries are fixed and the skatteavdrag follows the payroll recipes' simplification. The customer in Finland and the supplier in Denmark invoice in Swedish kronor, so there are no exchange differences. One verifikationsserie, A, is used for everything. Organisation numbers and momsregistreringsnummer are invented with a deliberately wrong check digit.
* **Simulator grading.** A booking is graded on accounts, sides and amounts; the verifikation date only has to fall in the month and the text only has to be present. The documents arrive one day at a time: a later document is shown only when every earlier one has a verifikation, even a wrong one. A rättelse is graded on the net effect of the original and all its rättelser, so a reversal and a new entry and a single difference entry are both accepted. Only the first booking of a document gives XP and counts for mastery. Återställ starts a new run with new amounts; the old run's verifikationer stay stored but are not shown.
* **Bankavstämning and month end.** The month end documents arrive by date and are not held back until the bank is reconciled; the missions only show the order. The marking is graded against the lines core's reconciliation leaves unmatched; ledger entries without a kontoutdrag line (a payment booked with a wrong amount) are fixed with a rättelse of that document, not in the avstämning. The badge "Månaden avslutad" is shown on the month summary; the Prestationer page that lists badges comes in D6.6. Accuracy per skill counts the first booking of each document.
* **AI guard.** The guard checks figures, legal references, length and language; it cannot check a claim that contains none of these, such as calling a balanced answer unbalanced. The system prompt forbids such claims, and the text is labelled as AI. English is recognised by common function words, so a short English phrase can pass. The daily count is advisory: two simultaneous first calls of a day may both be counted as the first. The cache lives in the process and is lost when the app restarts.
* **Tutor reach and wording.** The AI controls are under practice feedback only (Övningar, lessons, Repetition), not in the simulator. Qwen3-14B writes mostly correct but uneven Swedish; a sentence can lose its link to the one before ("Annars blir kostnaden för stor. Momsen kommer inte med i momsredovisningen."). The prompts ask for short answers built from the feedback's own reasons to keep this small; the text is labelled as AI and the built in feedback is always shown above it.
* **Nytt scenario.** Only on Övningar and only for ten domestic recipes; the simulator keeps its own counterparties (the simulator part of D6.3 was cut). The model's Swedish in the story is uneven: in a test of ten, one had a misspelling, and names can sound odd. The detail after the recipe's description can still be a poor fit for the category; only furniture, machines and computers under kontorsmateriel are refused automatically. The block list of well known companies is short, so a name can resemble a real company. Stories live in the session and are gone after a reload.
* **English lessons.** Only the sample lesson m3.l2 has an English version; the other lessons fall back to Swedish in English mode until D7.3.
