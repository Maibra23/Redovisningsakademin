# Limitations

Known gaps and simplifications. The full version is written in task D7; until then
every gap found during the build is added here.

* **Sparkod in the address.** Anyone who gets the link can open that progress. Accepted because no personal data is stored; the "Om appen och integritet" page says so.
* **PostgreSQL not yet verified end to end.** Storage and the learner flow were verified locally on SQLite. The check against Supabase PostgreSQL is the owner's deployment step after D1.5.
* **Settings actions not yet in the interface.** "Om appen och integritet" mentions resetting and deleting progress under Inställningar. The service functions exist (D1.4); the page is still a placeholder.
* **Moms rate date.** The legal rule uses the date of the beskattningsgrundande händelse; the engine uses the date of the transaction given to it. For the exercises these are the same date.
* **Momsomföring in öre.** `vat.settlement` moves the exact amount in öre to 2650. Rounding the momsdeklaration to whole kronor with öresutjämning on 3740 is not modelled.
* **Moms report period.** `vat.report` and `vat.settlement` count verifikationer by date within the given period and leave out omföringar; the caller must pass the period bounds, otherwise the whole journal is one period.
* **EU trade.** Only purchases and sales between businesses under the huvudregel, at 25 procent, are modelled. Trepartshandel, kedjetransaktioner, sales to private persons (OSS), the periodisk sammanställning and the 12 and 6 procent rates on EU purchases are not.
* **Payroll.** Skatteavdrag is an input; tax tables are not modelled. Only the full arbetsgivaravgift is used; age based reductions and växa-stöd are not. Avgifter are computed in öre on the summed bruttolön, while the arbetsgivardeklaration works in whole kronor. The payment voucher moves money from the bank to the skattekonto (1630); the debitering of 2710 and 2731 against 1630 is a separate verifikation.
* **Depreciation.** Straight line per month only, with each month rounded down to whole öre and the remainder in the last month. Residual value, half year conventions and degressive plans are not modelled.
* **Bankavstämning matching.** Lines match only one to one on an equal amount within three days. A bank line that covers several ledger entries (or the reverse) is reported as unmatched.
* **Reports before the bokslut.** `balance_sheet` adds the period's result to Årets resultat because no closing entries (bokslutsverifikationer) are made. Lagerförändring is not booked: purchases of handelsvaror are expensed in full and the varulager keeps its ingående balans. Subheadings such as Anläggningstillgångar, Omsättningstillgångar and Kortfristiga skulder are not yet part of the report objects. Account 4535 (tjänster från EU) is placed under Råvaror och förnödenheter by its kontoklass; a service that is an overhead would belong under Övriga externa kostnader.
* **Recipe simplifications.** The skatteavdrag in the payroll recipes is a chosen share of the bruttolön (28, 30 or 32 procent) rounded down to whole kronor, not a skattetabell. The hyra recipe has no moms (a landlord that is not frivilligt skattskyldig). Momsbetalning is the debitering on the skattekonto (debet 2650, kredit 1630); the transfer from the bank to the skattekonto is the betalning_skattekonto recipe. Utlägg is credited to 2890 Övriga kortfristiga skulder, with 2820 Kortfristiga skulder till anställda accepted. Exercise dates fall in 2026.
* **Mastery and XP choices.** Only the first attempt on an exercise instance counts for mastery and XP, because the feedback after it shows the solution. A miss resets the repetition count, so a Befäst skill can fall back to Kompetent. The XP thresholds for the levels (250, 750, 1 500, 3 000) are the app's own choice; PRD section 9 names the levels only. Today's date for due dates and the svit is taken in Swedish time (Europe/Stockholm).
* **Hitta felet and Avstämning.** A planted wrong amount changes two rows, the largest row and its counterpart on the other side, because a booked verifikation must balance. Avstämning uses only customer and supplier payments plus one bank fee, one month at a time, and its differences are always one missing fee and one transposed amount.
* **Exercise grid.** An empty account cell in the verifikation grid shows Streamlit's grey placeholder "None", which cannot be changed in Streamlit 1.67. Only the sample lesson m3.l2 exists, with status utkast, so the Övningar page shows exercises only when `RA_SHOW_DRAFTS=1` is set; hitta felet and avstämning are graded by the service but no content uses them yet (D4).
